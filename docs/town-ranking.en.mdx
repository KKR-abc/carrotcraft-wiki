<Callout type="info" title="Purpose of this system">
The single purpose of this system is to **encourage growing new players**.
You can't win by just gathering members or stacking veterans. The towns that
attract, support, and retain new players are the ones rewarded.
</Callout>

Every Saturday evening, the **weekly Town Ranking** is announced.
The top 3 towns receive **Market Intervention rights**, allowing them to manipulate
prices of selected CarrotMarket items for one week.

## 🏆 How is the Ranking Decided?

Each town's score rises as its members **play normally on the server**.
You don't need to complete specific tasks. Just play as you like and you'll contribute.

<Callout type="warn" title="Scoring criteria is hidden">
**What activities give points and how much** is intentionally undisclosed.
The system rewards **balanced, varied play** rather than exploiting a specific metric.
</Callout>

## ✨ New Players Are Special

Players who joined the server recently contribute **massively more** to their town's score.
Active newcomers are the single biggest driver of a town's ranking.

This is why towns work hard to invite, support, and retain new players.
**New players are genuinely valuable to a town.**

The multiplier is based on **server first-join date** — switching towns does not reset it.
The exact contribution rate is not disclosed.

## 🥇 Top-3 Reward: Market Intervention

The top 3 towns can **inject funds** into the wallet of selected CarrotMarket items.
This makes those items **sellable at a higher price for everyone**.

| Rank | Items | Wallet Boost |
|------|-------|--------------|
| 🥇 1st | 2 items | **+35% wallet** |
| 🥈 2nd | 1 item | +30% wallet |
| 🥉 3rd | 1 item | +20% wallet |

### Intervention Flow

Every **Saturday 18:00 JST** is the single weekly transition point.

<Steps>
<Step>
### Saturday 18:00 — New Ranking Announced

The new ranking goes live.
</Step>
<Step>
### Application Window (1 week)

**Saturday 18:00 → following Saturday 18:00** — top-town mayors/vice-mayors apply at any time.
</Step>
<Step>
### Following Saturday 18:00 — Simultaneous Activation

Submission deadline & all confirmed applications activate simultaneously (previous week's interventions end at the same time).
</Step>
<Step>
### Lasts 1 Week

Each intervention lasts **1 week** from activation.
</Step>
</Steps>

### How to Apply

**Mayors and vice-mayors of any town always see the Market Intervention button**
when they open `/market`. If your town is in this week's top 3, the application
screen opens. Otherwise, you'll see an explanation and the current qualifying ranks.

<Callout type="warn">
Confirm your application before the **next Saturday 18:00 JST**.
Towns that miss this window forfeit their intervention rights for that week.
The fixed activation time prevents late-night surprises.
</Callout>

### What if Multiple Towns Pick the Same Item?

When multiple top towns choose the same item, **only the largest boost applies**.
Smaller boosts are ignored, and those towns lose their slot for that item.

| Example | Result |
|---------|--------|
| 1st (+35%) and 2nd (+30%) both pick Diamond | Only 1st's +35% applies; 2nd's slot is forfeit |
| 2nd (+30%) and 3rd (+20%) both pick Iron | Only 2nd's +30% applies; 3rd's slot is forfeit |

### Consecutive Win Penalty

<Callout type="warn">
If the same town wins 1st place multiple weeks in a row, the intervention effect
diminishes **gradually by -5% per consecutive week**. It does not jump to -20%
immediately — only after 5 consecutive 1st-place wins does the penalty reach
the maximum of -20%. Missing 1st place even once resets the counter back to 100%.
This keeps opportunities open for other towns.
</Callout>

### Item Cooldown

An item that has been intervened cannot be intervened again by **any town for 4 weeks**.
This prevents specific items from being permanently manipulated.

## 📊 Viewing Your Town's Total Score

Mayors of towns with **15 or more members** can view their town's total score
through a dedicated button inside the `/market` GUI.

Smaller towns will not see this button. The breakdown and formula are not disclosed.

## 💻 Commands

| Command | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| `/market` | Intervention button is always visible to mayors and vice-mayors. Mayors of 15+ member towns also see a button to view their total score. |

## ❓ FAQ

<Accordions>
<Accordion title="How do I earn points?">
A town's score grows the most when **new players are active across many different
aspects of the game**. Just play normally and try varied activities — that's the
most effective way to contribute.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Do AFK hours count?">
No. Points are awarded for **activity across many different aspects of the game**,
not idle time.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Does switching towns reset my multiplier?">
The new-player multiplier is based on your **server first-join date**, so it doesn't reset.
Contributions however go to your current town (the day you switch towns counts as 0 for both).
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="What happens during an active intervention?">
The price stays elevated for the full 1-week intervention period.
**Sellers benefit during this window.**
Once the 1 week ends, the price returns to normal.
</Accordion>
</Accordions>
