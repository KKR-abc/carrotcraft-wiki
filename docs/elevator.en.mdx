<Callout type="info" title="On this page">
Stack two **iron blocks** vertically and **jump to go up / sneak to go down**. Works between 3 and 100 blocks apart, in both the Overworld and the Nether.
</Callout>

## 🛗 What is the Elevator?

By placing **iron blocks** vertically aligned (two or more), you can **jump or sneak** while standing on one to instantly travel between floors. Convenient for tower-style towns and tall buildings.

---

## 🪜 How to Use

<Steps>
<Step>
### Place an iron block on the floor

Place an **iron block** at the bottom floor.
</Step>
<Step>
### Place another iron block directly above

Place a second **iron block** at a higher Y, sharing the **same X,Z coordinates**.
</Step>
<Step>
### Jump to teleport up

Stand on the lower iron block and **jump** to teleport up.
</Step>
<Step>
### Sneak to teleport down

Stand on the upper iron block and **sneak** to teleport down.
</Step>
</Steps>

For multi-floor builds, place an iron block at every floor along the same column to use as elevator floors.

---

## 📋 Specifications

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Block | Iron Block |
| Min distance | 3 blocks or more |
| Max distance | 100 blocks or less |
| Up | Spacebar (Jump) |
| Down | Shift (Sneak) |
| Worlds | Main world & Nether |

### Conditions

| Condition | Detail |
|---|---|
| Headroom | The 2 blocks above the destination iron block must be passable (air, signs, water, etc.) — so the player has space to stand |
| X,Z alignment | The two iron blocks must share the same X,Z coordinates |
| Spacing | They must be 3 to 100 blocks apart |

<Callout type="info">
**It's fine to have other blocks between the iron blocks.** The plugin only checks the destination iron block and the 2 blocks of space above it. Floors, walls, and decoration in between are no problem.
</Callout>

---

## ⚠️ Notes

- Cannot be used in spectator mode
- Works in survival, adventure, and creative modes
- In creative, **flying takes priority over the elevator** — fly mode may prevent activation

---

## ❓ Troubleshooting

<Accordions>
<Accordion title="Q. Jumping on the iron block does nothing">
- The iron blocks may be **less than 3 blocks apart**
- Check that they share the **same X,Z coordinates**
- The 2 blocks of space **above** the destination iron block must be clear
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Q. Can't go down">
- Use **sneak (left Shift)** while standing on the upper iron block
- Don't combine jump and sneak — that may not register
</Accordion>
</Accordions>
