### 🛗 Elevator

Place **iron blocks** vertically aligned, then **jump or sneak** while standing on one to move between floors.

[Read more →](/en/docs/elevator)

### 🏠 Home & Tutorial

`/home` opens your personal home GUI — register up to 5 teleport points by default (more purchasable with cc). `/tpa` lets you request teleports to other players.

New players go through a **Tutorial** at spawn the first time they join.

[Read more →](/en/docs/home)

### 🌍 Random Teleport (RTP)

Use `/rtp` to randomly teleport to unexplored land. Won't drop you into other players' towns.

[Read more →](/en/docs/rtp)

### 🪑 GSit (Sit Down)

Right-click stairs and slabs to sit. Various poses and emotes also available.

| Command | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| `/sit` | Sit in place |
| `/lay` | Lie down |
| `/crawl` | Crawl |
| `/bellyflop` | Belly-flop |
| `/spin` | Spin |

To stand up, **sneak** or run the same command again.

## 🔒 Protection

<Cards>
  <Card icon={<Lock />} href="/en/docs/bolt" title="Chest Protection (Bolt)" description="Sneak + right-click twice to lock chests, doors, furnaces, and more." />
</Cards>

### 🔒 Chest Protection

Lock chests, doors, furnaces, and more so other players can't access them.
**Sneak + right-click twice** to quick-lock.

<Callout type="warn" title="Town admins can bypass">
Even with protection, the **mayor, co-mayor, and assistants** of the town containing the chest can view and break it. The most reliable theft prevention is to build your own town and place chests inside its borders.
</Callout>

[Read more →](/docs/bolt)

## ⚔️ Combat & Trading

<Cards>
  <Card icon={<Swords />} href="/en/docs/commands#pvp-commands" title="PvP Modes" description="Pick a combat style with /pvp mode. Damage only applies between matching modes." />
  <Card icon={<Gavel />} href="/en/docs/economy#auctions" title="Auctions" description="Bid on items with /auction. Listings from 5,000cc, 5% fee." />
</Cards>

### ⚔️ PvP Modes

Choose your combat style with `/pvp mode crystal / mace / sword / potion / all`. Damage only applies when **both players' modes allow the attack** (e.g. crystal vs crystal allows explosions; crystal vs sword is melee only). You can't change modes mid-combat (15s cooldown); your current mode shows on the action bar.

[Read more →](/en/docs/commands#pvp-commands)

### 🥕 Auctions

An **English-style auction house** for player-to-player sales. List items via `/auction` (from 5,000cc, 10% listing fee, up to 3 listings), bid via GUI or `b <amount>`. Outbid players get instant full refunds; collect winnings from `/auction box` (held 7 days, 5% fee).

[Read more →](/en/docs/economy#auctions)
