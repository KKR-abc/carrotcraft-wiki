<Callout type="info" title="🐔 ChickenBomber">
An asymmetric PVP game where players are split into Bombers and Survivors. Survivors dodge explosions while collecting "**Legendary Eggs**," and Bombers try to eliminate all Survivors with explosive chickens.
</Callout>

## 📖 Game Flow

### 1. Collection Phase (First Half)

<Tabs items={['🥚 Survivors', '💥 Bombers']}>
<Tab value="🥚 Survivors">

- **Dodge & Collect** — Avoid Chicken Bomber explosions and collect "Legendary Eggs" that appear at blast sites
- **Chests** — Obtain Legendary Eggs and potions from chests scattered across the stage
- **Healing** — While holding **5+ eggs**, continue sneaking to recover health
- **Phase Transition** — When the total eggs held by all Survivors exceeds a threshold, the "Counter-Attack Phase" begins

</Tab>
<Tab value="💥 Bombers">

- **Place Bombs** — Set Chicken Bombers to try to eliminate all Survivors
- **Blast Range** — Explosions deal damage in a **cross-shaped area**
- **Chain Detonation** — When an explosion hits another Chicken Bomber, it detonates faster than normal

</Tab>
</Tabs>

### 2. Counter-Attack Phase (Second Half)

<Tabs items={['🏹 Survivors', '💥 Bombers']}>
<Tab value="🏹 Survivors">

- **Revival** — Eliminated Survivors respawn with **1 HP**
- **Fight Back** — Survivors receive a crossbow
- **Sniping** — Hold the crossbow and **sneak (about 2 seconds)** to load an egg, then fire. The trajectory is a **straight line**

</Tab>
<Tab value="💥 Bombers">

- **Vulnerable** — Bombers teleport into the stage and can now take damage from Survivors
- **Resupply** — Obtain additional Chicken Bombers from chests for a final push

</Tab>
</Tabs>

## 📋 Important Points

| Item | Role | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Stamina System | Movement | Running continuously drains stamina and reduces movement speed. Walk or stop to recover |
| Egg Drop | Survivor | When a Survivor is eliminated, they drop half their eggs on the spot |
| Resupply Speed | Bomber | Bomber's bomb resupply time changes based on how many eggs Survivors have collected |
| Max Capacity | Bomber | Bombers can hold up to 6 Chicken Bombers at a time |

## 🥚 How to Obtain Legendary Eggs

| Source | Type | Description |
|---|---|---|
| From blast sites | pickup | Pick up eggs that appear after Chicken Bomber explosions |
| From chests | pickup | Open chests scattered across the stage |
| From eliminated players | pickup | Pick up half of the eggs dropped by eliminated Survivors |

## 🔧 How to Start

<Steps>
<Step>
### Gather at the waiting area
Players assemble in the lobby or waiting area.
</Step>
<Step>
### Choose Bomber preference
In the waiting area, you can choose whether to **volunteer as a Bomber**.
</Step>
<Step>

Due to player balancing, some players may be assigned as **CPUs**.
</Step>
<Step>
### Match begins
Starts in the collection phase, then transitions to the counter-attack phase based on progress.
