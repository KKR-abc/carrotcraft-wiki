<Callout type="info" title="🐑 MagicalSheep">
A unique PVP game where you fight using sheep as weapons. Collect items scattered across the stage while shooting sheep at opponents to deal damage. Each sheep has a special ability, and using them strategically is the key to victory.
</Callout>

| Mode | Type | Description |
|---|---|---|

| Solo Mode | Solo | The last player standing wins |

## 📋 Controls

<Tabs items={['Item Collection', 'Launch Sheep', 'Ability Activation', 'Arrow Detonation']}>
<Tab value="Item Collection">

Open chests on the stage to obtain various types of sheep and other items. **The number of sheep available is adjusted based on the number of players.**

</Tab>
<Tab value="Launch Sheep">

Select a sheep from your inventory and perform the "**drop item**" action to launch it as an attack.

</Tab>
<Tab value="Ability Activation">

When a sheep hits an enemy or obstacle, its unique special ability activates. Different sheep have different effects, such as **explosion damage** or **poison**.

</Tab>
<Tab value="Arrow Detonation">

**Hit a sheep with an arrow** to trigger its ability mid-flight. This enables precision-timed tactical plays.

</Tab>
</Tabs>

## ⚠️ Important Points

<Callout type="warn" title="Before you fight">
- **Player Scaling** — The number of sheep available changes based on player count
- **Diverse Items** — Your inventory includes other items like swords
- **Strategy** — Understanding sheep types and abilities for situational use is crucial
</Callout>
