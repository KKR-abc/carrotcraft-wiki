<Callout type="info" title="What is the Bug Hunter Program?">
A program that awards a **limited badge** and a **bounty (up to 1,000,000cc)** to members who find and report bugs. Reports of all sizes are welcome 🙌
</Callout>

## 🎁 Rewards

<Cards>
  <Card icon={<Bug />} title="Bug Hunter badge" description="A limited badge marking your contribution to the program" />
  <Card icon={<Coins />} title="Bounty up to 1,000,000cc" description="Awarded based on the nature and severity of the bug" />
</Cards>

## 📋 How to Report

<Steps>
<Step>
### Find a bug

Note any glitch or unexpected behavior you run into. Confirming that you can **reproduce it** makes review smoother.
</Step>
<Step>
### Post reproduction steps to the Support Center

Post the reproduction steps and context in as much detail as possible to the **Support Center** on [Discord](https://discord.gg/EKUPDxXaqb). Screenshots or recordings speed up the review.
</Step>
<Step>
### Review → badge & bounty awarded

Staff review the nature and severity of the bug and, if it qualifies, award the **badge and bounty**.
</Step>
</Steps>

## ✍️ Tips for a Good Report

<Cards>
  <Card icon={<FileText />} title="List the reproduction steps" description="In the order of 'what → did what → result'" />
  <Card icon={<Search />} title="Add context" description="Date/time, world, coordinates, items used, etc." />
  <Card icon={<Bug />} title="Attach evidence" description="Screenshots or recordings are ideal" />
  <Card icon={<MessageCircle />} title="One bug per report" description="Splitting them up speeds up review" />
</Cards>

## ⚠️ Notes

<Callout type="warn">
- The badge and bounty **vary by the nature and severity** of the bug. Not every report qualifies.
- Bugs that are already reported or known may not be eligible.
</Callout>

<Callout type="error" title="Exploiting bugs is prohibited (results in a BAN, not a bounty)">
**Exploiting a bug instead of reporting it** is strictly prohibited. Abuse such as item duplication (dupe) results in **punishment or a BAN**, not a bounty. See "Exploiting Bugs / Item Duplication" in the [Server Rules](/en/docs/rules).
</Callout>

## 📮 Where to Report

Found a bug? Report it freely via the [Discord Support Center](https://discord.gg/EKUPDxXaqb)!
