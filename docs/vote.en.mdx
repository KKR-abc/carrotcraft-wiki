## 🎁 Opening the Vote Crate

<Steps>
  <Step>
    **Receive a key by voting**

    After voting, a **Vote Key (Echo Shard)** is added to your inventory.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    **Right-click** to open a Gacha Key.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    **Spin the roulette**

    The reward is decided by a **roulette**.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    **Collect your reward**

    Won items / coins are automatically added to your inventory or balance.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warn">
  A full inventory may prevent you from receiving item rewards. Make space before opening keys.
</Callout>

## 📅 Consecutive Record Rewards
As you continue to vote each day, your record will accumulate. You will receive rewards on the 7th, 14th, 21st, and 30th days.

| Days | Reward |
| ------ | ------ |
| 7th | 10,000cc |
| 14th | Weapon Gacha Key and Armor Trim Gacha Key ×2 |
| 21st | Rare Item Gacha Key and Armor Trim Gacha Key ×3 |
| 30th | Silence Armor Trim Smithing Template |

<Callout type="info">
If you have protection tickets, your voting streak will be maintained even if you miss voting for a number of days equal to the number of tickets you possess.
 Protection tickets may sometimes be distributed by the management.
</Callout>

## 🎉 VoteParty
For every 1,000 additional votes across the entire server, a **Party Gacha Key** will be distributed to every player on the server.
Party Gacha Keys contain other types of gacha keys.

## ❓ FAQ

  <Accordion title="What if I don't use a key?">
    Vote keys persist between sessions and never expire.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I vote on the same site multiple times in a day?">
    No — each site has a **24-hour cooldown**.
  </Accordion>
