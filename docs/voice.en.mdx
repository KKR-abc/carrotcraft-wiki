<Callout type="info" title="Quick summary">
On **Bedrock or Java, without any mod**, you can talk to nearby players from your browser. The range is the same as the in-game voice chat (Simple Voice Chat), and you can talk with mod users too.
</Callout>

## 🎙 What is Web Voice?

Open the voice page in a browser on your phone or PC, and talk to players who are near you in the game. Bedrock players (Switch, PlayStation, mobile, etc.) can use it too.

- No mic? You can still join and **just listen**
- Nobody's position is shown. Only people you actually hear are listed

---

## 🪜 How to use

<Steps>
<Step>
### Type `/vc` in game

A login **QR code or link** appears (on Bedrock, the QR is shown as a map in an item frame).
</Step>
<Step>
### Open the QR or link and press "Log in"

This logs you in to the website. Once you are logged in, you can skip this step for a while.
</Step>
<Step>
### Press "Connect" on the voice page and allow the mic

You can now talk to nearby players. If you don't allow the mic, you join as a listener.
</Step>
</Steps>

**Next time**, just open the [voice page](https://carrotcraft.net/en/voice) while you are in the server and press "Connect".

---

## 🎛 The screen

- **Mic**: tap to turn on / mute
- **Speaker**: tap to listen / stop listening
- **Hold to talk**: when on, your voice is sent only while you hold the button (Space key on PC)
- **Speaking**: only people you hear are listed. You can change each person's volume or mute them
- **Groups**: join the same groups as the in-game voice chat (some have a password)
- **Settings**: mic / speaker device, AI noise suppression, stereo, buffer for choppy audio, and more

## ⌨️ In-game commands

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/vc` | Show the login QR / link |
| `/vc mute` | Mute / unmute your Web mic |
| Sneak + swap hands (F) | Mute / unmute your Web mic |
| `/vc off` | Disconnect Web Voice |

---

## 📱 Phones and consoles

- **Switch / PlayStation players**: open the voice page on a phone or PC
- **Playing Minecraft on a phone**: on some phones (especially iPhone) the mic stops while Minecraft is in front. Using **a second device** works best
- Supported browsers: Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari (iOS 14.5 or later on iPhone)

## 🎧 Simple Voice Chat (mod) users

<Callout type="info" title="Web Voice takes over while you are connected">
If you have the mod and then connect to Web Voice, **your mod mic and ears pause while Web Voice is connected** (so you don't hear everything twice). Disconnect Web Voice and the mod works as before.
Mod-only players are unaffected, and mod users and Web Voice users can talk to each other.
</Callout>

## ❓ Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| "You are not in the game" | Join the server first, then press "Connect" |
| "Wrong or expired code" | Type `/vc` again and use the new QR / link |
| Disconnected after changing servers | Press "Connect" again on the voice page |
| Others can't hear you | Check that "Mic" is on and the right mic is selected in Settings. On phones, a second device is recommended |
| Audio is choppy | In Settings, set "When choppy" to "More buffer" |
| Someone is too loud / quiet | Change just that person's volume in the "Speaking" list |
