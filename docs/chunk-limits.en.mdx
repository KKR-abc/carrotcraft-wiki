<Callout type="info" title="About chunk limits">
To maintain server stability, there are limits on the number of blocks and entities that can be placed per chunk (16×16 blocks). Placement or spawning is restricted once the limit is reached.
</Callout>

## 🐾 Entity Limits

Limits for entities (mobs, items, etc.). The check counts the chunk **plus its surrounding chunks (a 3x3 chunk area)**.

There are no per-species limits — entities are capped by **category totals** (villagers are the one exception with an individual cap).

| Category | Total Limit |
| --- | --- |
| Animals | 70 |
| Monsters | 140 |
| NPC | 60 |
| Water Mobs | 20 |
| Ambient | 20 |
| Other (armor stands, item frames, etc.) | 500 |

<Callout type="info" title="Villagers only: individual cap">
Villagers tend to concentrate in trading halls, so they have an individual cap of **40 per area** in addition to the category total.
</Callout>

## ⚠️ When Limits Are Reached

<Callout type="warn" title="Excess entities are auto-removed">
**Blocks**: You will be unable to place more and a notification will appear on screen.

**Entities**: Excess entities are automatically removed. Named entities are prioritized for preservation.
</Callout>

<Callout type="info" title="Design tip">
When building large devices, spreading them across multiple chunks helps avoid hitting limits.
</Callout>

## ❓ FAQ

<Accordions>
<Accordion title="How do I see chunk borders?">
Press F3 + G (Java Edition) to show chunk borders. One chunk is 16×16 blocks.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Are named mobs always safe?">
Named entities are prioritized for preservation, but they can still be removed if the limit is heavily exceeded.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Can the limits be raised for me?">
For performance reasons, individual exceptions are not granted. Please spread your build across multiple chunks instead.
</Accordion>
</Accordions>
