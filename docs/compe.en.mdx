<Callout type="info" title="What is the Weekly Competition?">
Compete against other players for one week based on a specific theme—such as the number of items mined or harvested.
The top **3 players** receive a cash prize.
</Callout>

## 🏃 Past Themes

### 1st: "Diamond Mining Count"
### 2nd: "Successful Fishing Count"
### 3rd: "Crop Harvest Count"
### 4th: "Stone-type Block Mining Count"
### 5th: "Ancient Debris Mining Count"

## 🏆 Rewards

The top 3 winners of the weekly competition receive **medals** and **cash prizes**.
| Rank | Prize |
|---|---|
| 1st | 50,000cc |
| 2nd | 25,000cc |
| 3rd | 10,000cc |

## Uses for Medals

<Steps>
<Step>

### Exchange for Cash

Collect 10 medals to exchange them for 200,000cc.

</Step>
<Step>

### Exchange for a Grail

Collect 3 medals to exchange them for a **Coal Grail**.  
Combine two Grails of the same rank to craft a Grail of the next rank up.  
Coal → Bronze → Iron → Gold → Diamond → Emerald → Lapis → Redstone

</Step>
</Steps>

## 👀 Checking Rankings and Details

You can check the rankings and details by using commands in-game.
`/compe`

<Callout type="warn" title="Rankings hidden near the end">
Rankings will no longer be visible as the competition nears its end. If you want to check your current standing, be sure to do so early.
</Callout>

## ⚠ Important Notes
- For mining or harvesting tasks, immediately collecting items you placed yourself does not count toward the total.
- The start time of the competition is not announced in advance.
- Be aware that rules may be added or updated.
