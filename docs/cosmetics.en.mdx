  Cosmetics are **available only to VIP and Media rank players**. See the [VIP page](/docs/vip) for how to obtain VIP rank.
</Callout>

<Callout type="info" title="What are Cosmetics?">
  **Decorative items** that change your appearance. These do not affect combat or stats — purely visual.
</Callout>

## 🎒 How to Open

```
/uc menu
```

This opens the main menu. Choose a category and click a cosmetic to equip it.

## ⌨️ Main Commands

| Command | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| `/uc menu` | Open the main menu |
| `/uc clear` | Unequip all cosmetics |

## ❓ FAQ

<Accordions>
  <Accordion title="Do cosmetics make me stronger?">
    No. Cosmetics are **purely visual** — they don't affect combat, stats, or anything mechanical.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Other players become invisible or laggy">
    Heavy use of particle cosmetics can cause performance issues. Try unequipping cosmetics from the menu.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A cosmetic is broken">
    Report bugs or broken cosmetics to staff.
  </Accordion>
</Accordions>
