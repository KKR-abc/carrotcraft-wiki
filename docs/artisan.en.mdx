## 🧰 How to use

<Steps>
  <Step>
    **Open `/assyuku`**

    The Artisan hub opens: balance, delivery box, price list, and the frontier shop.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    **Put materials in the delivery box and close it**

    Everything is delivered the moment you close it. **Shulker boxes work too** — the contents get delivered and the box is returned.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    **Buy from the frontier shop**

    Choose Move (slide the border) or Reset (rebuild the world). Your purchase is announced to every server.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## 🧱 How compression works

Every accepted material can be **compressed on a crafting table**. Fill a 3×3 grid with 9 of the same item and you get one compressed block, one tier up. You can stack this up to **7 tiers**.

<Steps>
  <Step>
    **9 in, 1 out**

    Put 9 of the same material into a crafting table (fill the whole 3×3) to get one tier-1 compressed block.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    **Stack it up**

    Nine tier-1 blocks make one tier-2 block, and so on — **up to tier 7**.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    **Unpacking gives 8 back**

    Put a compressed block alone in a crafting table and it becomes 8 of the tier below.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warn" title="A round trip costs you one item">
  Compressing is **9 → 1**, unpacking is **1 → 8**. Go both ways and you lose one item (the compression tax). A compressed block is worth exactly what it unpacks into (tier 1 = 8 items, tier 7 = 2,097,152 items).
</Callout>

<Callout type="error" title="Never place a compressed block, never craft with it">
  A compressed block looks exactly like the plain material but holds up to millions of items. **Placing it destroys that value.** Only compress, unpack, or deliver it — the plugin blocks it from ordinary recipes.
</Callout>

## ⚖️ Efficiency — compressing before delivery costs you

How much you get paid depends on **how many times the item has been compressed**.

| What you deliver | Type | Efficiency |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Plain, uncompressed material | spec | **100%** |
| Tier 1–6 | spec | Drops linearly as the tier goes up |
| Tier 7 (max) | spec | **75%** |
| Anything inside a shulker box | spec | Flat **75%**, whatever the tier |

<Callout type="warn" title="In other words">
  **Delivering plain, uncompressed material pays the most.** Compression exists for hauling and for compression ranks/badges — not as a way to earn more points. Dumping a whole shulker is convenient, and that convenience costs a flat 25%.
</Callout>

## 🗺️ Frontier work — Move and Reset

The resource world has a **border**. You can slide it, or rebuild the world from scratch.

### Move (slide the border)

The border keeps its **size** and slides in the direction you choose. Fresh land opens ahead; the land behind falls outside.

| Item | Type | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Unit | spec | 1 unit = 1,000 blocks. Up to 20 units per purchase |
| Direction | spec | Frontline / North / East / South / West |
| Takes effect | spec | **1 hour** after purchase |
| Next purchase | spec | **2 hours** from the moment you buy |

### Reset (rebuild the world)

<Callout type="warn">
  **Buying a Reset is temporarily disabled.** The purchase screen shows "currently disabled" and the option cannot be bought.
</Callout>

The resource world is **regenerated with brand new terrain**. The border returns to its starting position.

| Item | Type | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Takes effect | spec | **2 hours** after purchase |
| Next purchase | spec | **5 hours** from the moment you buy |
| Your items | spec | Inventory, XP and ender chest are **kept** |
| Your builds | spec | Anything placed in the resource world is **gone** |

<Callout type="error" title="Do not keep valuables in the resource world">
  Both Move and Reset change the terrain. **Chests and builds do not come back.** Keep your base on the main server and use the resource world for mining.
</Callout>

***

## ⏰ Announcements and countdown

Every purchase is **announced on all servers** — who bought it, which world, which direction, and how far.

The countdown runs at **45m → 30m → 15m → 10m → 5m → 2m → 1m → 30s**, then **10 → 5 → 3 → 2 → 1**. **Use that time to haul your loot out.**

<Callout type="info" title="You are moved somewhere safe during a reset">
  When a reset executes, anyone in the resource world is moved to a **safe empty space**. You take no damage and cannot fall. When it finishes you are **teleported into the new world** automatically.
</Callout>

## ❓ FAQ

<Accordions>
  <Accordion title="I delivered items but nothing happened">
    The material may not be **accepted**, or you may have hit the **daily cap**. Rejected items are returned to you. Your remaining cap is shown in the `/assyuku` hub.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I convert points into money?">
    No. Compression Points are only good for frontier work. You can **send** them to other players (`/assyuku pay`).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I bought it but nothing moved">
    Move takes effect after 1 hour, Reset after 2 hours. That delay exists so everyone can **haul their loot out**. Check the remaining time by running `/carrotborder eta` in the resource world (it is not available on the main server).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens to my base in the resource world?">
    Land that falls outside after a Move, and land rebuilt by a Reset, **never comes back**. The resource world is for mining. Keep valuables on the main server.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The price changed since yesterday">
    Rates move with **how much has been delivered**. Heavily delivered materials get cheaper and recover once people stop. Check the price list for the current rate.
  </Accordion>
</Accordions>
