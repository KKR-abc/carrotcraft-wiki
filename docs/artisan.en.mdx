<Callout type="info" title="What is the Artisan?">
  Turn your leftover stone and dirt into **Compression Points**, then spend them to **expand or rebuild the resource world**. When the resource world runs dry, the players fix it themselves.
</Callout>

## 📦 In short

<Cards>
  <Card title="1. Deliver materials" description="Drop cobblestone, iron, diamonds and more into the delivery box and close it. You earn Compression Points (pt)." />
  <Card title="2. Buy frontier work" description="Spend points to Move the resource world border, or Reset the world entirely." />
  <Card title="3. Everyone benefits" description="Your purchase is announced server-wide and executed at a set time. Fresh land opens up for everyone." />
</Cards>

<Callout type="warn" title="Points are not money">
  Compression Points **cannot be converted to CC (currency)**. They are only good for resource world frontier work.
</Callout>

## 🧰 How to use

<Steps>
  <Step>
    **Open `/assyuku`**

    The Artisan hub opens: balance, delivery box, price list, and the frontier shop.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    **Put materials in the delivery box and close it**

    Everything is delivered the moment you close it. **Shulker boxes work too** — the contents get delivered and the box is returned.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    **Buy from the frontier shop**

    Choose Move (slide the border) or Reset (rebuild the world). Your purchase is announced to every server.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## 🪙 Delivering materials

| Item | Type | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Accepted items | spec | Cobblestone, stone, deepslate, dirt, sand, gravel, netherrack, logs, coal, iron, gold, copper, redstone, lapis, quartz, emerald, diamond, and more |
| Rejected items | spec | Named, enchanted, or custom items are returned to you untouched |
| Shulker boxes | spec | Contents are delivered; the box itself comes back |
| Daily limit | spec | There is a daily cap on how much you can deliver. The remaining amount is shown in the hub |
| Reset time | spec | 4:00 AM (JST) every day |

<Callout type="info" title="How prices are decided — each material has a quota">
  Every material has a **daily quota that is bought at full price**.

  - While the quota lasts, you get the **full price**
  - Once it runs out, you get **half price**
  - Once that runs out too, that material is **done for the day** (it sells again tomorrow)

  The quota **refills gradually over time**, so there is no morning rush to be first in line.

  If everyone delivers the same material, its quota runs out early. Materials **nobody has been delivering** still have quota left, so those pay better. Check the current rate in the **price list** inside `/assyuku`.
</Callout>

## 🧱 How compression works

Every accepted material can be **compressed on a crafting table**. Fill a 3×3 grid with 9 of the same item and you get one compressed block, one tier up. You can stack this up to **11 tiers**.

<Steps>
  <Step>
    **9 in, 1 out**

    Put 9 of the same material into a crafting table (fill the whole 3×3) to get one tier-1 compressed block.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    **Stack it up**

    Nine tier-1 blocks make one tier-2 block, and so on — **up to tier 11**.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    **Unpacking gives 8 back**

    Put a compressed block alone in a crafting table and it becomes 8 of the tier below.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warn" title="A round trip costs you one item">
  Compressing is **9 → 1**, unpacking is **1 → 8**. Go both ways and you lose one item (the compression tax).
</Callout>

<Callout type="error" title="Never place a compressed block, never craft with it">
  A compressed block looks exactly like the plain material but holds thousands of items. **Placing it destroys that value.** Only compress, unpack, or deliver it — the plugin blocks it from ordinary recipes.
</Callout>

## ⚖️ Compressing now pays MORE

It used to cost you to compress before delivering. **That is reversed now.** Deliveries count the number of items that went **into** the compression, plus a bonus based on the tier.

| What you deliver | Type | What you get |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Plain, uncompressed material | spec | **100%**, as-is |
| Tier 1 | spec | **+11%** (9 items compressed once = 10 items' worth) |
| Tier 2 | spec | **+22%** |
| Tier 3 | spec | **+33%** |
| Tier 4 | spec | **+44%** |
| Tier 5 and above | spec | **+50%** (caps here) |
| Anything inside a shulker box | spec | Flat **75%**, whatever the tier — **no bonus** |

<Callout type="warn" title="In other words">
  **Compressing by hand before you deliver pays the most.** Dumping a whole shulker is convenient, and that convenience costs you: a flat 75% with no bonus. The exact points per tier are shown in the `/assyuku` price list.
</Callout>

## 🗺️ Frontier work — Move and Reset

The resource world has a **border**. You can slide it, or rebuild the world from scratch.

### Move (slide the border)

The border keeps its **size** and slides in the direction you choose. Fresh land opens ahead; the land behind falls outside.

| Item | Type | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Unit | spec | 1 unit = 1,000 blocks. Up to 20 units per purchase |
| Direction | spec | Frontline / North / East / South / West |
| Takes effect | spec | **1 hour** after purchase |

### Reset (rebuild the world) — by reservation

You no longer pick the time. A reset always happens at **18:00 (JST) on the day it is booked**.

| Time | Type | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The moment you buy | spec | The reservation is locked in and **that world closes** — nobody can enter while the new terrain is prepared |
| 15:00 | spec | **Booking deadline** for that day. Later purchases roll over to the next day |
| 17:00 | spec | The new world goes live — **only the buyer and the people they picked** can enter |
| 18:00 | spec | **Open to everyone.** The border also starts expanding, reaching full size over 30 minutes |

<Callout type="warn" title="One reservation per day, first come first served">
  You cannot stack reservations on the same day. **The world closes the moment a reservation is made**, so never leave valuables in the resource world — lost items cannot be restored.
</Callout>

### Bringing people in early

The buyer can choose who joins them at 17:00. Run `/frontier reserve`, then click the heads you want to add (**up to 20 people**). You get to gather supplies together before the world opens to everyone.

### The dragon egg

The End is **reset by staff on the 1st of every month**. **Only that dragon drops the egg** — dragons in a purchased reset do not.

| Item | Type | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Your items | spec | Inventory, XP and ender chest are **kept** |
| Your builds | spec | Anything placed in the resource world is **gone** |

<Callout type="error" title="Do not keep valuables in the resource world">
  Both Move and Reset change the terrain. **Chests and builds do not come back.** Keep your base on the main server and use the resource world for mining.
</Callout>

## 🏘️ Saving points as a town

`/frontier town` lets you use your **town wallet**.

- **Deposit** — any resident of the town
- **Withdraw** — the mayor and the helpers the mayor picks

Pool your points and buy frontier work as a town.

***

## ⏰ Announcements and countdown

Every Move purchase is **announced on all servers** — who bought it, which world, which direction, and how far.

The countdown runs at **45m → 30m → 15m → 10m → 5m → 2m → 1m → 30s**, then **10 → 5 → 3 → 2 → 1**. **Use that time to haul your loot out.**

A Reset is announced when it is booked, and the switch-over times are fixed (see the table above).

## ❓ FAQ

<Accordions>
  <Accordion title="I delivered items but nothing happened">
    The material may not be **accepted**, or you may have hit the **daily cap**. Rejected items are returned to you. Your remaining cap is shown in the `/assyuku` hub.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Should I compress before delivering?">
    Yes. Anything you compressed by hand earns a bonus that grows with the tier (capped from tier 5). But **items dumped inside a shulker box get no bonus** — those are a flat 75%.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I convert points into money?">
    No. Compression Points are only good for frontier work. You can **send** them to other players (`/assyuku pay`).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I bought a Reset but nothing changed yet">
    Resets run at **18:00 (JST)** on the day they are booked (deadline 15:00; later purchases roll to the next day). The buyer and their guests get in from 17:00. A Move takes effect 1 hour after purchase — check the time left by running `/carrotborder eta` in the resource world (it is not available on the main server).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens to my base in the resource world?">
    Land that falls outside after a Move, and land rebuilt by a Reset, **never comes back**. The resource world is for mining. Keep valuables on the main server.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The price changed since yesterday">
    Each material has a daily quota that sells at full price; once it is gone the price halves, and once that is gone the material stops selling for the day. The quota refills over time. Check the price list for the current rate.
  </Accordion>
</Accordions>
