## 🐴 About Horse Training

On CarrotCraft, horses can become far more capable than vanilla. **Train** them to boost stats, **breed** them across generations, and inherit rare **traits** with special abilities.

Supported mobs: **Horses, Skeleton Horses, Zombie Horses** (camels disabled).

---

## 📈 Stat Caps (vs. Vanilla)

| Stat | Type | Description |
|---|---|---|
| HP | stat | Vanilla max 30 HP → CarrotCraft max 300 HP |
| Movement Speed | stat | Vanilla max 0.337 → CarrotCraft max 0.4 |
| Jump Strength | stat | Vanilla max 1.0 → CarrotCraft max 1.2 |

---

## 🎒 Horse Items

Horses can be carried in your inventory as a **saddle item**.

- **Right-click** the item to summon the horse
- The item displays **gender, stats, growth, training progress, and traits**
- If you never want to lose your horse, store it as an item in a safe place

---

## 🌱 Growth System

Foals reach **adulthood after 3 minutes**. Adult horses are **1.3× vanilla size**.

---

## 💪 Training System

Riding, brushing, and feeding your horse improves **3 separate stat categories** permanently.

<Tabs items={['Riding', 'Brushing', 'Feeding']}>
<Tab value="Riding">

### 🐴 Riding

Distance ridden = riding progress. **+0.2% speed per 1% progress**.

| Unit | Amount |
|---|---|
| 100 blocks ridden | +1% progress |
| Per 1% progress | +0.2% speed |

</Tab>
<Tab value="Brushing">

### 🪮 Brushing

Use a **brush** on your horse. **+0.2% jump per 1% progress**.

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Per brush use | +2 units |
| Units per 1% | 1 unit |
| Cooldown | 180 sec |
| Per 1% progress | +0.2% jump |

</Tab>
<Tab value="Feeding">

### 🍎 Feeding

Feed your horse. **+0.2% max HP per 1% progress**.

| Food | Food Value |
|---|---|
| Wheat / Apple / Carrot / Sugar | 1.0 |
| Hay Block | 2.0 |
| Golden Carrot | 3.0 |
| Golden Apple | 5.0 |
| Enchanted Golden Apple | 8.0 |

Cooldown 180s. **2.0 total food value = +1% progress**.

</Tab>
</Tabs>

<Callout type="info">
Training bonuses are **permanent for that horse only** — they are NOT inherited by offspring. Each horse must be trained individually.
</Callout>

## 🧬 Breeding & Mutation

A child's stats are **the average of both parents ± a mutation range**:

| Stat | Mutation Range |
|---|---|
| Health | ±3 HP |
| Speed | ±0.05 |
| Jump | ±0.05 |

### Breeding Rules

- **Opposite-gender pairs only** (same-gender breeding disabled)
- **Males** have no breeding cooldown
- **Females** have a **5-minute breeding cooldown**

### Tips

<Steps>
<Step>
### Collect spawn horses

Catch horses with strong stats whenever you find them.
</Step>
<Step>
### Pair an opposite-sex couple

One male + multiple females is efficient.
</Step>
<Step>
### Train every horse separately

Bonuses don't pass to children, so train each one individually.
</Step>
<Step>
### Goal

Max out HP 300 / Speed 0.4 / Jump 1.2.
</Step>
</Steps>

## 🏘 Towny Integration

Horse actions inside towns require **town permissions**:

- Spawning horses
- Breeding
- Interacting with horses

You may be restricted in towns you don't belong to.
