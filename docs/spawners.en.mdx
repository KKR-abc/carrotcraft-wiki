Use a **Silk Touch pickaxe** to collect mob spawners at **100% success rate** and place them anywhere. Without Silk Touch, the spawner is destroyed permanently.
</Callout>

## 📦 What is Spawner Pickup?

On CarrotCraft, you can collect dungeon **mob spawners** without destroying them. Place them somewhere else to set up a custom mob farm.

---

## ✅ Pickup Requirements

| Requirement | Type | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Silk Touch | required | Mine with a Silk Touch pickaxe |
| Pickaxe | required | Must mine with a pickaxe (any tier) |
| Success Rate | rate | 100% |

<Callout type="warn">
Mining a spawner with a pickaxe **without Silk Touch** destroys it. Always use Silk Touch when collecting.
</Callout>

---

## 🧱 Placement

<Steps>
<Step>
### Hold the spawner item

Take the collected spawner item from your inventory.
</Step>
<Step>
### Click the side of a block

Click the side of any block to place the spawner there.
</Step>
<Step>
### Check the mob type

The mob type stored in the spawner item is shown in its **item text** (e.g., "Spawner — Zombie").
</Step>
</Steps>

---

## ⚠️ Notes

- Spawners are destroyed by explosions (not recoverable)
- Vanilla 1.13+ keeps spawners as their original mob type — collect them on sight
- Use **end rods** to suppress spawning while moving them home
