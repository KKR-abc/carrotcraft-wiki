<Callout type="info" title="How to read this page">
**Land Protection (CarrotGuard)** lets you protect your land **by area**. Select two corners with a wooden hoe and create the protection via `/guard`. This is separate from [chest protection](/docs/bolt) (single containers) and [Towny](/docs/towny) (whole towns).
</Callout>

## 🛡 What is Land Protection?

It protects an **area you outline with a wooden hoe**, preventing other players from breaking, placing, or stealing inside it. There are two types.

<Cards>
<Card title="Beginner Protection" description="Free protection usable for a limited period after joining. A starter tool to protect your first base." />
<Card title="Town Protection" description="Created by mayors and assistants. The size cap grows with your resident count, and it costs money." />
</Cards>

<Callout type="warn" title="Do not protect others' builds without permission">
Enclosing another player's build or land within your own protection **without their permission is prohibited**. If discovered, the protection will be **forcibly removed** and the offender **subject to punishment** (Rules 3.1).
</Callout>

## ✏️ How to create

<Steps>
<Step>
### Get a wooden hoe

Selection uses **a plain wooden hoe**. Just craft one and hold it (no special item is handed out).
</Step>
<Step>
### Select two points

Holding the wooden hoe, **left-click for point 1** and **right-click for point 2**. The selected area is shown as a particle outline (the full height is always included).
</Step>
<Step>
### Create with `/guard`

Run `/guard` and click **"Create from selection"**. On the confirmation screen, check the **type (beginner / town)**, size, cost, and eligibility, then **confirm**.

<Callout type="info" title="Switch type if you qualify for both">
If you qualify for both (e.g. a mayor/assistant still within the beginner period), use the button at the top of the confirmation screen to switch between **beginner ⇔ town** protection.
</Callout>
</Step>
</Steps>

## ⚙️ Protection settings (toggles)

`/guard` → click a protection → **Settings (toggles)** lets you switch the following ON (restricted) / OFF (allowed) for that protection.

| Toggle | When ON |
|---|---|
| Block breaking | Non-members cannot break blocks |
| Block placing | Non-members cannot place blocks |
| Use / open chests | Non-members cannot use/open |
| Attack (PvP / animals) | PvP and animal damage banned inside |
| Monster spawning | Monsters won't spawn inside |
| Fire spread | Fire won't spread |

Break, place, and use are ON (protected) by default.

---

## 👥 Adding members

From a protection's management screen → **Add member**, you can add people who may build with you.

- **Online players**: click their head from the list
- **Offline players**: use the **anvil** button to type a name (players who haven't joined yet can also be specified)

## 🚫 Where you cannot create protection

Protection cannot be created in the following places/situations.

<Accordions>
<Accordion title="Common (both types)">
- Overlapping an existing protection or a **Towny town plot**
- Where a **town already exists nearby (within 150 blocks)**
- On another player's build/land (unauthorized protection breaks the rules)
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Beginner protection">
- The beginner period (14 days after joining) has **expired**
- Exceeding the **max count (3)** of beginner protections
- Exceeding the **max size (32×32)**
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Town protection">
- You are **not a mayor or assistant**
- The town's total protected area exceeds the **size cap (residents ÷ 2 × 256 blocks²)**
- You **don't have enough money (CC)**
</Accordion>
</Accordions>

## 🗑 When protection is removed

<Callout type="warn" title="Beginner protection disappears automatically">
Beginner protection is **automatically removed after a fixed period (14 days) from joining**. You'll be warned as the deadline approaches. Before it disappears, **create a town** or **have an existing town protect you**.
</Callout>

- **Beginner expiry**: auto-removed 14 days after joining
- **Overlapping a town plot**: protection later overlapped by a Towny plot is auto-removed
- **Manual deletion**: management screen → Delete → confirm on the confirmation screen
- **Rule violations**: protections confirmed as violations (e.g. protecting others' builds) are forcibly removed by staff

<Callout type="info" title="Griefing can be recovered">
Even if a protection is lost and the area is griefed, staff **may be able to restore it via rollback** if you report it ([Compensation & Rollback](/docs/rules#6-補填ロールバック)).
</Callout>

## 📋 Commands

| Command | Description |
|---|---|
| `/guard` | Protection menu (list / create / manage) |
| `/guard tool` | Info on how to select (using a wooden hoe) |
| `/guard create` | Create from your selection (without opening the GUI) |
| `/guard list` | List your own protections |

Admins have `/guardadmin` (list, info, teleport, flag toggles, delete, and more).
