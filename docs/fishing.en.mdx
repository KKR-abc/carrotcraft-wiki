## Getting started

<Steps>
<Step>
### Cast into water

Right-click with a fishing rod and make sure the bobber is floating.

</Step>
<Step>
### Reel in when the bobber sinks

Watch the bubbles and right-click as soon as the bobber dips.

</Step>
<Step>
### Check your catch

Look at the fish name, rarity, and size in your inventory.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Sell fish

```text
/emf shop
```

Use the sell GUI. To sell all EMF fish in your inventory:

```text
/emf sellall
```

## Journal and bait

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/emf journal` | Opens the fish journal |
| `/emf applybaits` | Applies bait to the held fishing rod |
Fishing competitions are not currently being held.
