## 🛒 How to Get a Rank

<Tabs items={['💴 Donate (PayPay)', '🎬 YouTube Shorts (free balance)']}>

<Tab value="💴 Donate (PayPay)">

<Steps>

<Step>
### Contact the Support Center on Discord
Open a ticket at the **Support Center** on [CarrotCraft Discord](https://discord.gg/EKUPDxXaqb). **No need to decide a rank yet** — just tell us how much you want to deposit.
</Step>

<Step>
### Generate a PayPay "Receive Link" and paste it in the ticket
**Easiest method:** create a fixed-amount receive link in your PayPay app, then paste the URL into the ticket. The yen amount becomes your in-game balance 1:1.

**Step-by-step (PayPay app)**:
1. Open PayPay → tap "**送る (Send)**" on the home screen
2. Tap "**受け取りリンクを作成する (Create receive link)**"
3. Enter the amount you want to send (e.g. ¥500)
4. Tap "**○○円を送る**" → a receive link (URL) is generated
5. Copy the URL and paste it into the Discord ticket

**Examples**:
- ¥500 → balance of ¥500 (1 month of VIP)
- ¥3,000 → balance of ¥3,000 (1 month of MVP+ or 6 months of VIP)
- Any amount works (e.g. ¥200 buys 4 gacha keys)

> 💡 **The receive link is valid for 4 days (96 hours).** If staff doesn't claim it in time, the amount is **automatically returned to your balance** — no risk of losing the money.
</Step>

<Step>
### Staff manually credits your balance
Usually within 24 hours. You'll get a notice in the ticket. **At this point no rank is granted yet** — only the balance is topped up.
</Step>

<Step>
### Pick a rank anytime in-game
`/cpremium buy <vip|mvp|mvpplus>` or click the rank icon in `/uc menu` → confirmation dialog. **The monthly fee is then deducted from your balance and the rank is granted.**

> 💡 Once you have balance, you're free to decide: upgrade to MVP later, extend VIP next month, save for a big spend — your choice.
</Step>

</Steps>

  **Differential upgrades**: VIP (¥500) → MVP (¥1,000) only charges the **¥500 difference**.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warn">

</Callout>

## ❓ FAQ

<Accordions>

<Accordion title="What happens when my rank expires?">
  - Donation ranks (VIP/MVP/MVP+) **auto-expire after 30 days**
  - After expiry: drops back to default rank
  - Gacha wins are kept as **owned** and instantly become usable again when you re-subscribe
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I downgrade from MVP+ to VIP?">
  **Downgrade purchases are blocked.** Wait until your rank expires, then re-subscribe at the desired tier.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How is differential upgrade calculated?">
  We charge the **difference** between the current and target rank.
  Example: VIP (¥500) → MVP (¥1,000) costs **¥500**.

  The new rank gets a fresh 30 days, and at expiry you'll fall back to your previous VIP (stored as the "underlying" rank).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I apply the YouTube reward multiple times for the same video?">
  No — **one submission per video**. You can't re-apply after view count grows.
  Instead, **make new videos** — there's no limit on the number of submissions.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Will fake view inflation be caught?">
  Yes. Obvious bought views or bot traffic are not counted. We'll **reject the application** on detection.
  Repeat offenders may be banned from future submissions.
</Accordion>

</Accordions>
