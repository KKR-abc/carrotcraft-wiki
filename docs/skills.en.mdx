<Callout type="info" title="Quick summary">
Skills grow from **what you already do** — breaking blocks, harvesting crops, fighting mobs. Leveling up unlocks **abilities**, and Mining and Farming also **pay you directly**.
</Callout>

## 📈 What are skills?

CarrotCraft has **11 skills**. There is nothing to set up: just play normally and they level up on their own.

Open the menu with `/skills`.

| Skill | How it levels | Pays money |
|---|---|---|
| **Mining** | Breaking stone and ores | ⭕️ |
| **Farming** | Harvesting crops | ⭕️ |
| **Foraging** | Breaking logs and leaves | ✖️ |
| **Excavation** | Digging dirt, sand, gravel | ✖️ |
| **Fishing** | Catching fish | ✖️ |
| **Archery** | Killing mobs with a bow | ✖️ |
| **Fighting** | Killing mobs in melee | ✖️ |
| **Defense** | Taking damage | ✖️ |
| **Agility** | Taking fall damage | ✖️ |
| **Alchemy** | Drinking and brewing potions | ✖️ |
| **Enchanting** | Enchanting, anvils, grindstones | ✖️ |

## 💰 How income works

**Only Mining and Farming** pay money, and it arrives the moment you break the block. More valuable ores pay more.

| Block | Payout |
|---|---|
| Ancient Debris | 20 CC |
| Diamond Ore | 10 CC |
| Lapis Ore | 3 CC |
| Gold Ore | 2 CC |
| Emerald Ore | 2 CC |
| Iron Ore | 1.5 CC |
| Copper / Redstone Ore | 1 CC |
| Coal Ore | 0.5 CC |
| Wheat / Beetroot | 0.5 CC |
| Nether Wart | 1 CC |
| Carrot | 0.4 CC |
| Potato | 0.35 CC |

Stone, netherrack, logs and dirt give **XP only** — no money.

### Earnings taper past 10,000 CC per day

So that a handful of players cannot break the economy by grinding, **once your daily income passes 10,000 CC your share gradually decreases**.

| Income that day | Your share |
|---|---|
| Up to 10,000 CC | **100%** |
| 15,000 CC | 50% |
| 20,000 CC | 20% |
| 25,000 CC | 10% |

<Callout type="info">
This resets at **midnight Japan time (JST)**, back to 100%. Normal play rarely reaches 10,000 CC, so most players never notice it.
</Callout>

Your current share is shown in the `/skills` menu.

## ✨ Abilities unlocked by leveling

<Steps>
<Step>
### Farming — Auto Replant (Lv5+)

Harvesting a crop **replants it automatically**, so you never have to plant it back by hand.

The chance rises with your level: **25% at Lv5, up to 100% at Lv100**. When the roll fails, **no seed is consumed**.
</Step>

<Step>
### Foraging — Extra Drop (Lv1+)

Breaking a log sometimes **drops one extra**. The chance rises to **25% at Lv100**. It is independent of Fortune, so both apply.
</Step>

<Step>
### Mining — Haste (Lv10 / 25 / 50)

You sometimes gain the Haste effect. Cooldown is 10 minutes.

| Level | Effect |
|---|---|
| Lv10 | Haste I (15s) |
| Lv25 | Haste II (15s) |
| Lv50 | Haste II (25s) |
</Step>

<Step>
### Fighting / Defense / Agility — Passive buffs

| Skill | Effect | Max at Lv100 |
|---|---|---|
| **Fighting** | Damage dealt | +5% |
| **Defense** | Damage taken | -5% |
| **Agility** | Movement speed | +20% |

These scale gradually with your level.
</Step>
</Steps>

## ⚠️ What does not count

A few things are excluded so skills cannot be farmed unfairly.

- **Blocks you placed yourself** — place-and-break loops give nothing
- **Blocks moved by pistons**
- **Breaking a lot at once** — from the 33rd block onward XP drops to 25%
- **Anvil work that only renames an item** — Enchanting only counts when enchantments increase or durability is restored
- **Grindstone use on unenchanted items**

## ❓ FAQ

**Q. Am I missing out if I ignore skills?**

No. They level up naturally as you play. There is no need to grind them deliberately.

**Q. Is there a level cap?**

No cap, but each level needs more XP than the last.

**Q. Where does the money go?**

Straight into your server balance (CC). Check it with `/bal`.

**Q. My income seems to have dropped.**

Check `/skills` to see whether you passed 10,000 CC today. It resets at midnight JST.
