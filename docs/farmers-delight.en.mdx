## 📅 New recipes unlock daily!

<Callout type="info">
Addon recipes (sushi, corn dishes, desert sweets...) unlock automatically, **3 per day at 18:00 JST**, announced on **Discord**.

- **Locked recipes can't be cooked yet** (stockpiling ingredients ahead is fair game!)
- Core Farmer's Delight dishes and workbench crafting are **available from the start**
</Callout>

## 🔪 Using the stations

<Steps>
<Step>
### Cooking Pot 🍲

Place the pot **over a fire** (campfire / stove), right-click with ingredients to drop them in. Cooking starts automatically when a recipe matches; right-click with a **bowl** (or other container) to serve.
</Step>
<Step>
### Cutting Board + Knife 🔪

Place food on the board and **right-click with a knife** (or axe / shovel) to process it. You can also hold the tool in your main hand and food in the off-hand to cut in one motion.
</Step>
<Step>
### Stove & Skillet 🔥

The **stove** cooks food laid on top and acts as a heat source for the pot. The **skillet** is a frying pan that cooks quickly over a fire.
</Step>
<Step>
### Bamboo Mat 🎋

A no-fire station for sushi and onigiri. Right-click ingredients onto the mat; collect the result **bare-handed** with a right-click. Craft: String ×2 + Bamboo ×4.
</Step>
<Step>
### Furnace / Smoker / Campfire ♨️

Prepped slices can be cooked in a vanilla **furnace, smoker or campfire** (smoker is 2× faster, campfire is slower).
</Step>
</Steps>

## 💧 Glass of Water — a drink that makes you hungry 🆕

<Item img="/items/cc/glass_of_water.png" name="Glass Of Water" /> is an unusual new item: **drinking it makes you hungrier on purpose**, so you can **taste-test your way through lots of dishes**.

### 🤔 What's it for?

CarrotCooking's hearty dishes (soups, stews, feasts) grant Farmer's Delight's **Nourishment** effect — for a while, **your hunger bar stops draining entirely**. Great… but it also means **you can't eat the next dish while you're full or still Nourished**. Cooking a dozen recipes you couldn't actually try was the old problem.

Enter the **Glass of Water**: drink it to **free up your hunger bar and move on to the next plate** — just like washing down a big meal with water in real life.

### 🍴 Effect

| | |
|---|---|
| **Craft** | <Item name="Water Bucket" /> + <Item name="Glass Bottle" />×4 → <Item img="/items/cc/glass_of_water.png" name="Glass Of Water" />×4 (milk-bottle style split; 4 at once) |
| **Drink** | Hunger **−3 drumsticks** (6 points) + **clears the Nourishment effect** |
| **Bottle** | The **glass bottle is returned** when finished (craft it again forever) |
| **Stacking** | Stacks up to 16 — easy to carry a supply |

<Callout type="info">
A vanilla **water bottle** (plain water from a brewing stand) works too, more gently (hunger **−1 drumstick** + clears Nourishment). If you run out of Glasses of Water, a water bottle is a fine substitute.
</Callout>

### 💡 Tips

- **For taste-testing:** pop one when you want to try newly-unlocked or freshly-cooked dishes back to back.
- **To cancel Nourishment:** while Nourished you can't even eat healing food — a Glass of Water clears it instantly.
- **Don't overdo it:** at 0 hunger you'll eventually start taking damage (same as vanilla). Just eat normally if you drop too low.

## 💡 Good to know

<Callout type="info">
- Hearty soups & feasts grant **Nourishment** (hunger doesn't drain). Drink a **Glass of Water** when you want to get hungry again.
- **Feasts & popcorn boxes** are served one portion at a time; breaking a half-eaten one returns a "**(N servings left)**" item — place it again to continue (no duping).
- Cakes and pies can be placed and **sliced with a knife**.
- Many dishes reproduce the original mod's effects (regen, resistance, speed...).
</Callout>
