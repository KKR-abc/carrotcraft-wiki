## 🛡 1. Account & Client Rules

Rules to maintain the fairness of game systems.

### 1.1 Cheat Clients & Mod Restrictions

The use of any tools or mods that enable actions impossible with the standard Minecraft client is prohibited.

<Callout type="error" title="Prohibited">
- Hack clients such as KillAura, X-Ray, Fly, Speed, etc.
- Automation tools (macros, taped-down keys, etc.)
- Printer functionality (auto-building)
- Cracking the world seed, or using seed information to gain unfair advantages (locating ores, structures, etc.)
</Callout>

<Callout type="info" title="Allowed (Exceptions)">
- Performance mods (OptiFine, Sodium, etc.)
- Japanese input mods
- Shader packs
- Minimap mods (limited to information equivalent to the web map)
- Inventory sorting mods
- Simple Voice Chat
</Callout>

### 1.2 Involvement with Rule Violators

Intentionally assisting rule violators (cheaters, dupe exploiters, etc.) or receiving benefits from their violations (illegitimate items, land) is prohibited. **You may receive the same or harsher punishment than the original violator.** Knowingly failing to report violations may also be considered aiding.

### 1.3 Bug & Exploit Abuse / Item Duplication

Intentionally exploiting server or plugin bugs/glitches for unfair advantage is prohibited. If you discover a bug, please report it to staff immediately instead of exploiting it. **Concealing a known bug without reporting it is also subject to punishment.**

<Callout type="error" title="The following are strictly prohibited">
- Using item duplication (dupe) exploits
- Intentional item duplication using TNT, pistons, or other mechanics
- Inventory manipulation duplication using chests, hoppers, etc.
- Rollback duplication exploiting server lag or crashes
- Knowingly receiving duplicated items
</Callout>

### 1.4 Custom Item Abuse

The following actions regarding MajinCraft custom items and Majin Ingots are prohibited:

- Duplicating (duping) custom items or obtaining them through exploits
- Inflating Majin Points through automated trading tools
- Bypassing anvil/furnace restrictions to modify custom items
- Using custom items on other players' land by circumventing Towny permissions

### 1.5 Account Limits

- **Alt accounts:** Allowed on a trial basis. Use **requires prior declaration via the Support Center**, and **alts may not join Towny**.
- **Unauthorized use:** Using an alt account without declaring it will result in a ban of that account. In severe cases, the main account may also be punished.
- **Also prohibited:** Using alternate accounts to evade punishment, double-claiming vote rewards.

### 1.6 Connection Requirements

For anti-griefing and security reasons, connecting through VPNs or proxy servers is prohibited.

## 🤝 2. Community & Code of Conduct

Guidelines to prevent player conflicts. This section applies to both in-game chat and Discord.

### 2.1 Harassment

Discriminatory remarks regarding race, gender, religion, nationality, etc., excessive verbal abuse, threats, sexual harassment, and impersonation are prohibited. Continued behavior after being warned by the affected party will result in harsher punishment.

### 2.2 Privacy Protection

Sharing or publishing other people's personal information (real name, address, photos, contact info, IP address, etc.) is strictly forbidden, regardless of whether they have given permission. This includes sharing the content of private messages (`/msg`, etc.) without the sender's consent.

### 2.3 Spam & Advertising

Chat spam, meaningless shouting, and advertising other servers are prohibited. Posting invite links to external Discord servers is also prohibited.

<Callout type="info">
**However, sharing Discord links related to CarrotCraft (such as town/nation Discord servers) is allowed.** Intentionally damaging the server's reputation through false or exaggerated claims is also prohibited.
</Callout>

### 2.4 RMT (Real Money Trading)

Buying or selling in-game data (items, currency, land, accounts, etc.) for real money, or soliciting such transactions, is strictly forbidden.

### 2.5 Inappropriate Content

The following expressions are prohibited across all content including MCIDs, item names, signs, map art, and builds:

- Sexual, violent, or grotesque content (NSFW)
- Defamation and discriminatory expressions
- Problematic political or religious content
- Any other content that may cause discomfort to other players

### 2.6 Inappropriate Skins

The use of skins that cause discomfort to others (sexual, violent expressions, etc.) or transparent/semi-transparent skins is prohibited.

### 2.7 References to Other Servers and Other Games

**Talking about other servers or other games is prohibited** in CarrotCraft chat and on the official Discord. This applies to casual conversation as well.

<Callout type="error" title="Prohibited">
- Bringing up other servers or other games as a topic of conversation
- Soliciting or advertising other servers or other games
- Comparison-baiting such as "Server X is better," intended to provoke conflict
- Criticizing other servers, or excessively praising them
- Soliciting players to move to or merge with another server
- Asserting a specific outside community ("I'm from Server X")
- Posting URLs, invite codes, or IP addresses that encourage joining external servers
</Callout>

<Callout type="info">
"Other servers" here means servers outside CarrotCraft. CarrotCraft's own servers (Nation, Resource, Casino, and so on) and communities for towns and nations within CarrotCraft are **not** covered by this rule.
</Callout>

## 🔨 3. Gameplay & World Protection

Rules regarding building, destruction, and combat.

### 3.1 Griefing & Nature Protection

- Destruction and placement of obstructive blocks on or near other players' land is prohibited.
- **"Destruction" includes not only block destruction but also killing other players' mobs (villagers, livestock, etc.).**
- **"Other players' land" refers to any area recognized as being used or managed by another player, regardless of whether it is protected by Towny.** Areas where builds or facilities exist are treated as other players' land even without Towny protection.
- Even in unprotected wilderness, actions that significantly damage the landscape (lava casting, etc.) or large-scale destruction of natural terrain (mountain removal, landfill, etc.) are prohibited.
- Pointless terrain destruction with no legitimate purpose (randomly digging holes, indiscriminately stripping the ground, chopping down trees and leaving the mess, etc.) is prohibited regardless of scale.
- Pointless destruction of naturally generated structures (villages, temples, ruins, etc.) with no legitimate purpose is likewise prohibited.
- **Protecting another player's builds or land (e.g. setting up land protection over them) without their permission is prohibited.** Enclosing someone else's build within your own protection is treated as hijacking/obstruction. If discovered, the protection will be forcibly removed and the offender subject to punishment.

### 3.2 Theft & Asset Management

- Illegally taking items from other players' chests is prohibited. Joining a town for the purpose of stealing is also prohibited.
- Asset management is the player's own responsibility. It is recommended to store valuables in Ender Chests.
- Compensation for theft is determined based on staff investigation but is not guaranteed.

### 3.3 PvP Rules

<Callout type="warn" title="Enabling PvP is entirely at your own risk">
Staff will not provide any compensation or support for item loss or deaths that occur while PvP is enabled.
</Callout>

**Traps:**
- Traps that harm other players (fall traps, lava traps, etc.) are **only allowed against players who have PvP ON.** Traps targeting PvP OFF players are prohibited.

<Callout type="error" title="Prohibited actions">
- **PvP OFF attacks:** Using lava, water, flint & steel, fall damage, etc. to indirectly kill players who have PvP disabled.
- **Spawn camping:** Waiting at respawn points (homes, town spawns) to repeatedly attack players immediately after they respawn.
- **Safe zone abuse:** Exploiting bugs or mechanics in PvP-protected areas (lobby, etc.) to harm players.
</Callout>

### 3.4 Server Load Restrictions

Actions that cause excessive server load are prohibited:

- Large-scale redstone circuits (always-on clock circuits, etc.)
- Devices that generate large numbers of entities
- Excessively large mob farms or auto-farms

If in doubt, consult staff on Discord beforehand. Staff may stop or remove devices without prior notice.

### 3.5 AFK Restrictions

- The use or creation of mechanisms to intentionally bypass automatic AFK kick (AFK machines, macros, etc.) is prohibited.
- The use or creation of auto-fishing machines (auto-fishers) is prohibited. Auto-fishing by holding down or repeatedly clicking manually (including click-locking and click-spamming) is also prohibited.

### 3.6 Ownership in Shared Areas

Traps and builds placed in shared areas — such as the End main island and End gateway portal areas — **do not grant exclusive ownership rights.** This means no one may monopolize the *location*; it does **not** make the structures themselves unowned. Traps and builds you place there remain yours.

- You have no right to restrict other players' movement or mining near your traps in shared areas. Issuing warnings such as "stay away" or forcibly excluding players is prohibited.
- However, intentionally destroying or sabotaging other players' traps is prohibited.
- If you wish to remove another player's build, **you must contact them first.** Unauthorized destruction may be treated as griefing.
- Even for builds placed outside Towny protection, staff will investigate and respond if griefing, destruction, or theft is confirmed.

### 3.7 Casino Machine Usage

Do not operate a machine (slot, card table, etc.) that another player is using. **A player is still using the machine during win animations and while choosing a bet.**

- Any winnings obtained by taking over another player's machine are **subject to confiscation**.
- **Hogging a machine is also prohibited** — refusing to let others use a machine you are not actively playing, or ignoring people waiting their turn.
- Machines have a short occupancy timer, but it is only a supporting mechanism. **Exploiting gaps in that mechanism is treated as a violation of this rule.**

Evidence is required for compensation claims. We recommend keeping recordings or screenshots.

### 3.8 Map Art

**Distribution, Display & Trading:** Displaying in item frames, trading, and buying/selling map art **with in-game currency** is freely permitted. It is recommended to keep screenshots as evidence in case of trade disputes. (Selling for real money is prohibited per [2.4 RMT](#24-rmt-real-money-trading).)

## 🏛 4. Towny Rules

Rules for using Towny features.

### 4.1 Naming Rules

Town and nation names must use alphanumeric characters only. Japanese characters and inappropriate names that violate public order and morals are prohibited.

### 4.2 Land Claims & Obstruction

Surrounding other towns to block their expansion or acquiring land for the purpose of obstruction is prohibited.

<Callout type="error" title="Prohibited">
- Claiming exclaves (territory not connected to the main town by land)
- Surrounding other towns with your own land to prevent expansion or access
- Mass-placing protected chests around other towns
</Callout>

**Exception:** If the other party intentionally expanded near your territory and became surrounded as a result, this rule may not apply.

### 4.3 Evicting Residents

When evicting residents from a town, administrators must provide time for item collection. However, immediate eviction is permitted for griefers or severe rule violators.

## ⚖️ 5. Staff Policy

### 5.1 Staff Authority

The staff team has final authority on all server matters. Actions not explicitly listed in these rules may still result in punishment if deemed detrimental to server order.

### 5.2 Communication Monitoring

In-game chat (including `/msg`, etc.) may be reviewed by staff for the purpose of preventing misconduct. Players should understand that these communications are not completely private.

### 5.3 Harassment Toward Staff (Customer Harassment) Policy

CarrotCraft staff members volunteer their time to maintain a safe and enjoyable environment for all players. Based on the Japanese Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare's "Customer Harassment Countermeasure Manual," the following actions are defined as harassment and will be dealt with strictly (the items below are examples and not exhaustive).

<Callout type="error" title="Prohibited actions">
- **Psychological attacks & personal denigration:** Threats, intimidation, coercion, slander, defamation, insults, abusive language, denial of personhood, discriminatory remarks, sexual harassment or stalking against staff, and equivalent conduct.
- **Reputation damage & impersonation:** Damaging the reputation of the server or staff through false information or rumors; defamation or privacy violations against individual staff on social media or the internet; impersonating staff or fabricating/distorting staff decisions; recording, filming, or publishing inquiries, DMs, or calls without permission.
- **Excessive or unreasonable demands:** Prolonged confinement of staff time, persistent/repeated inquiries, rehashing already-answered matters or excessively repeating the same request, derailing from one's own violation, inciting other players to pressure staff, demands for compensation/apology/staff punishment without reasonable grounds, demands for compensation beyond the scope of these rules, and demands for service that are socially unreasonable.
</Callout>

**Response policy:**

When the above actions are confirmed, staff will respond as follows:

1. **Warning:** The individual will be asked to cease the behavior.
2. **Mute/BAN:** If no improvement is shown after warning, or if the behavior is severe, immediate punishment will be applied.
3. **Legal action:** In cases of threats, defamation, etc., evidence will be preserved and legal action or reports to authorities may be taken.

### 5.4 Rule Amendments

These rules may be amended without prior notice to maintain a healthy play environment. Players are responsible for regularly checking and following the latest rules.

### 5.5 Disclaimer

- Staff bears no responsibility for disputes or damages between players.
- Staff bears no responsibility for damages caused by service suspension or termination.

## 🔄 6. Compensation & Rollbacks

### 6.1 Compensation

In cases of griefing or theft, items will only be returned to victims if they can be recovered from the violator. Full compensation is not guaranteed.

### 6.2 Rollbacks

In cases of large-scale griefing, the affected area may be rolled back to its pre-incident state.

<Callout type="warn">
- Rollbacks are not intended as item compensation; items lost due to rollback will not be reimbursed.
- Causing a rollback through false reports will be severely punished.
</Callout>

## ⚒️ 7. Punishments

When rule violations are confirmed, the following punishments may be applied at staff discretion. Punishment severity is determined by the nature of the violation, frequency, and degree of remorse. Warnings accumulate and are never reset.

| Punishment | Type | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Warning (Warn) | punishment | A caution for minor violations. Escalates if no improvement is shown. |
| Mute | punishment | Restricts chat usage for a set period or indefinitely. Applied for verbal abuse or spam. |
| Kick | punishment | Forcefully disconnects from the server. May serve as a temporary warning. |
| Confiscation | punishment | Removal of illegally obtained items, violation structures, or status resets. |
| Temp Ban | punishment | Prohibits server connection for hours to days. |
| Permanent Ban | punishment | Applied for severe violations (cheating, doxing, griefing) or repeated offenses. |
| IP Ban | punishment | Completely blocks access from a specific connection source. Circumvention via alt accounts is also blocked. |

### 💸 Fines

A fine is automatically deducted from your in-game balance (CC) alongside the punishment.

| Punishment | Fine |
|---|---|
| ⚠️ Warning (Warn) | 10,000 CC |
| 🔇 Mute (temporary) | 10,000 CC × days |
| 🔇 Mute (permanent) | 1,000,000 CC |
| 🔨 Temp Ban | 10,000 CC × days |
| 🔨 Permanent Ban | 1,000,000 CC |
| 👢 Kick | no fine |

- Fines are **deducted automatically** when the punishment is issued
- If your balance is insufficient, it goes **negative (debt)** — pay it off with income or `/pay` transfers
- A chat notice shows the punishment, amount charged, and remaining balance (shown on next login if you were offline)

### Immediate Permanent Ban

<Callout type="error" title="The following actions will result in an immediate permanent ban">
- **Cheat usage:** X-Ray, Fly hacks, and other tools that severely compromise fairness
- **Unauthorized sub-accounts:** Using sub-accounts without prior approval
- **Malicious griefing/attacks:** Intentionally causing damage to the server
</Callout>

### Appeals

Appeals against punishments must be submitted within **1 week** of the punishment notification via Discord's ticket system. Appeals submitted after this period will not be addressed. Continuing to protest after a punishment is finalized may result in additional punishment.

## 📮 8. Reports & Contact

Please report rule violations and trouble through the **ticket system** on [Discord](https://discord.gg/EKUPDxXaqb).

<Callout type="info" title="Reporting tips">
- Reports via chat or DM may not be addressed
- Please include **screenshots or recordings** whenever possible
- Reports should be submitted within **1 week** of the incident
- False reports and staged incidents are subject to punishment
- Tampering with or concealing evidence is prohibited
</Callout>
