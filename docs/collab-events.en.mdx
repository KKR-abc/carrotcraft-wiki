## 📌 How Collaborations Work

<Steps>
<Step>
### Submit your idea
Open a ticket with your event proposal (see required fields below).
</Step>
<Step>
### Staff review
Ideas submitted via ticket are **discussed and reviewed by the staff team**.
</Step>
<Step>
### Production
- If something **can't be built in survival or needs a plugin**, the staff team will create it
- Players good at **plugin or resource pack creation** are very welcome to contribute
</Step>
<Step>
### Flexible team composition
- Members of your own town
- A newly recruited production team
- Mixed staff + player teams
</Step>
<Step>
### Run the event
Schedule the date and host it. We'll do a retrospective afterwards.
</Step>
</Steps>

## 💡 What to Include in Your Proposal

Including the following in your ticket helps us evaluate your idea quickly.

| Item | Required | Description |
|---|---|---|
| 🎯 Overview | Required | What is the event / what happens |
| 👥 Participants | Required | Who can join (everyone / sign-up / per-town / etc.) |
| 🤝 Help needed | Required | What you'd like staff to build (structures / plugins / prizes) |
| 🗓️ Preferred timing | Recommended | Rough date or time window |
| 🏆 Prize ideas | Optional | If any (we can also figure this out together) |

## 📝 How to Submit

Open a new ticket from the ticket channel in [CarrotCraft Discord](https://discord.gg/EKUPDxXaqb) and include the items above. Staff will reach out to discuss your proposal.
