The **BreweryX** plugin lets you craft real drinks by cooking, distilling, and aging ingredients. Each drink is rated **â1â5** for quality, and drinking it makes you tipsy (the screen sways) and may grant **special effects** depending on the recipe.

## ð¶ The Brewing Process

<Steps>
<Step>
### Cook in a cauldron

Place a **water-filled cauldron** with a **heat source directly below it** (fire, lava, magma block, campfire, soul variants all work). **Right-click the cauldron** while holding an ingredient to add it.

- **Right-click the cauldron with a clock** to see the current cooking time (minutes)
- The particle color above the cauldron is also a timing hint
- When the recipe's **cooking time** is reached, right-click with **3 glass bottles** to bottle the liquid
</Step>
<Step>
### Distill (only for some recipes)

Place the bottles in the Brewing Stand, add Blaze Powder as fuel, and insert Glowstone Dust as an ingredient to begin the distillation process. The process completes after repeating the distillation the number of times specified in the recipe (the plugin automatically processes all bottles).  
The Glowstone Dust is not consumed.  
Recipes with **0 distill runs** (most beers, wines, and sakes) skip this step.
</Step>
<Step>
### Age in a barrel (only for some recipes)

Attach a sign reading **"barrel"** to a structure of wood blocks to create a **barrel**. Put bottles inside and let them age the required number of **years**.
There are two sizes of barrels. For the smaller version, arrange the blocks in a 2x2x2 cube and place stairs facing inward to form a cross shape; position a sign at the bottom right.
The larger version measures 3 blocks in width and height, and 5 blocks in depth. Use full blocks for the central section. Place fences at the four bottom corners of the barrel body to act as legs, and attach a sign to the center; place a fence beneath the sign.

- **1 "year" â 20 minutes** of real time
- The **wood type** affects quality â match the recipe's wood type for the best result
</Step>
<Step>
### Quality (â) is decided

The more precisely you match the recipe's ingredient amounts, cooking time, distill runs, aging years, and wood type, the more **â** you earn. Higher **difficulty** recipes are stricter.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warn" title="Watch out for failures">
Over-cooking, over-distilling, over-aging, or using the wrong wood lowers quality or produces an undrinkable "ruined" brew. Until you get the hang of it, **measure the time with a clock** and test in small batches.
</Callout>
